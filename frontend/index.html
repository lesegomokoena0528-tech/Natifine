Everything you need

One platform for the whole fine lifecycle

From the moment a fine is issued to the moment it is paid or disputed, NatiFine keeps every party informed.

Instant fine visibility

Motorists see every fine linked to their registered vehicles with amounts, due dates and supporting evidence.

Secure online payment

Settle fines through a simulated gateway, receive a reference number and download an official PDF receipt.

Transparent disputes

Contest a fine online, upload supporting documents and track the adjudication outcome end to end.

Vehicle registry

Keep your vehicles up to date so fines and notifications always reach the right owner.

Timely notifications

Automated reminders for due and overdue fines, payment confirmations and dispute decisions.

Enforcement analytics

Administrators and officers get dashboards covering fines, revenue, disputes and payment performance.
